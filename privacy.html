Starfinity Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Starfinity.

1. Developer and contact

Starfinity is developed and published by jens Armbruster, an independent developer.

Privacy enquiries:
j.armbruster92@gmx.de

2. Local game data

Starfinity is primarily an offline game. Game progress, campaign state, gameplay settings, locally generated player identifiers, display information, unlocked content, and related game data are stored locally on your device.

Starfinity does not currently require an online account and does not upload this game data to a Starfinity server. Save files are encrypted and protected against accidental corruption or modification on the device.

3. Custom commander portraits

You may voluntarily select an image from your device's photo library and crop it for use as a commander portrait. Starfinity requests access only when you choose this feature.

The application does not request the selected image's location or other photo metadata. The cropped portrait is converted to a local image and stored in Starfinity's application storage. It is not uploaded to a Starfinity server or provided to advertising services.

4. Advertising

Mobile versions of Starfinity may offer optional rewarded advertisements. You choose whether to view a rewarded advertisement in exchange for a clearly described in-game reward. Declining an advertisement does not prevent you from continuing to play the game.

Starfinity uses AppLovin MAX to request, display, measure, and prevent fraud in connection with advertisements. Depending on your device, location, permissions, consent choices, and the advertising partners enabled through MAX, AppLovin and its partners may automatically process:

  • Device manufacturer, model, operating system, and app version;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • Country, language, locale, and time-zone information;
  • Network connection and device configuration information;
  • Advertising identifiers, vendor identifiers, or similar device identifiers when permitted;
  • Advertising consent and tracking-preference signals;
  • Advertisement requests, impressions, completion events, interactions, and related performance information; and
  • Device-integrity, security, and fraud-prevention signals.

This information may be used to provide contextual or personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit repeated ads, produce reporting, detect invalid activity, and improve advertising services.

Starfinity does not intentionally send your commander portrait, local save contents, or chosen display name to AppLovin. Advertising placement data sent by Starfinity is limited to non-sensitive technical placement names, such as identifying the daily-login rewarded-ad placement.

For more information about AppLovin’s collection and use of your information visit: https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/ .

AppLovin may use additional advertising partners to provide ads. The current list is available from the AppLovin Partners page .

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, Starfinity presents a consent form before requesting personalized advertising or accessing an advertising identifier. You can decline personalized advertising and may still receive less-personalized or contextual advertisements.

Where available, you can review or change advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Settings option inside Starfinity. You can also change tracking and advertising permissions through your device settings.

6. Data sharing

The developer does not operate a system that sells or transfers your local save files or commander portrait. Advertising-related information may be processed and shared by AppLovin with advertising networks, advertisers, measurement providers, service providers, and other partners as described in AppLovin's privacy documentation.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data and custom portraits remain on your device until they are deleted, replaced, reset through an available in-app control, or removed through your device's application-data or uninstall functionality.

Because local game data is not uploaded to the developer, the developer generally cannot access or remotely delete it.

AppLovin controls the retention of information collected through its advertising services. AppLovin states that advertising-service information is generally retained for limited periods, typically up to two years, although some information may be retained for shorter or longer periods where required.

8. International processing

AppLovin and its advertising partners may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent.

You may contact the developer at j.armbruster92@gmx.de concerning information controlled by Starfinity.

Requests concerning information controlled by AppLovin should be made using the privacy tools and contact methods described in the AppLovin Privacy Policy .

You may also have the right to submit a complaint to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Starfinity is not directed to children as defined by applicable privacy laws. The developer does not knowingly use AppLovin advertising services in connection with a child. If you believe that a child has provided personal information or received advertising contrary to applicable law, please contact j.armbruster92@gmx.de.

11. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, request time, and pages visited to operate, secure, and maintain the service.

More information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Starfinity's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
